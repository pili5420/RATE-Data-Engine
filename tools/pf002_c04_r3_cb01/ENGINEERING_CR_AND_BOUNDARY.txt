Problem and resulting behavior
1. The original audit guard raised ONE_FIXED_NATIVE_ROOT_ONLY without exporting its
   predicate evidence. The new guard preserves exact builtin-string equality and
   captures closed, bounded metadata before allowing or refusing the same event.
   Unapproved observed values are never printed, hashed, serialized or coerced.
   The current code/tool/manifest hashes bind that receipt; it is not authorization.
2. The original cleanup unconditionally used self.hp even when CreateProcess had
   been rejected before returning an owned process handle. New explicit lifecycle
   states preserve CREATE/ASSIGN/RESUME unknown outcomes. Absence of hp cannot
   prove absence of a process. Only a not-attempted call or a recorded, validated,
   first STAGE_A rejection with zero prior accepted creates can support NO_ROOT.
3. NO_ROOT cleanup queries the same newly owned Job's ACCT/PID list only. Full,
   strict, consistent zero-process evidence is PENDING_CLOSE. EMPTY confirmation
   requires successful exact Job CloseHandle and all other resources closed.
   Unknown members, API/evidence/close failures never become a confirmation.
4. Suspended, unassigned known roots may be terminated by their retained owned
   handle once. Assigned roots use only the owned Job and retained known handle.
   Neither successful TerminateJobObject nor CloseHandle proves containment.
   Unknown assignment/resume outcomes are not silently reclassified.
   A returned thread handle is retained immediately. State/argument rejection is
   inside ownership protection; only the registered owned thread may be closed,
   never an arbitrary argument handle. Failed closes are recorded, not retried.
   Both paths share an intent-before-dispatch close-once function; bool, Job and
   process-handle aliases are rejected without a thread CloseHandle dispatch.
   Finalization requires exact phase/basis, matching process/thread/Job close
   evidence, and rejects root-derived resources in a NO_ROOT observation.

- Wrong command/CWD/type/arity/state/count: terminal audit refusal, no retry.
- Malicious observed values: builtin type categories only, no repr/str/equality
  on unknown objects, bounded lengths, hashes only of exact approved values.
- Receipt tamper/source changes: closed strict receipt plus actual source hashes.
- Missing handle or wrapper exception: unknown creation outcome, never empty PASS.
- PID reuse/unknown members: no unknown PID OpenProcess or individual termination.
- False cleanup PASS: raw accounting plus exact close evidence and full states.
- Partial evidence/close failure: UNKNOWN; no promotion from observation alone.
- Publication accidentally triggering Native: CB01 CLI disabled, offline workflow
  only, full workflow trigger audit and independent pre-publication review.
